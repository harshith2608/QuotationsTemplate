Everything in QuoteRoom

Quote in minutes

  • Room by room: sizes in ft & in, mm, cm or inches; area and totals as you type
  • Your materials and rates: laminate, acrylic, veneer, box or frame work, your own
  • Price by item or by material; fittings, plywood choice and discounts
  • Every change saved as a revision; go back to any one

Send & get it accepted

  • Two PDFs: an Initial Quotation (amount per room) and the Full Quotation
  • Your terms, payment terms and specifications on every PDF
  • WhatsApp in one tap, or email from your own address
  • A link in your colours: the customer views, downloads and taps Accept

Invoice & get paid

  • Quotation to invoice in one step: CGST + SGST or IGST, or without GST
  • Invoices numbered by financial year, shared as a link
  • Record advances and instalments; see what each customer owes
  • Razorpay payment links and polite WhatsApp reminders

Run the business

  • Every job by stage: awaiting, confirmed, ongoing, completed
  • Material list for each job: sheets, hardware, paint, to send to suppliers
  • Costs & profit on every quotation, and yearly reports (owner only)
  • Staff logins with view or edit access to the jobs you give them
