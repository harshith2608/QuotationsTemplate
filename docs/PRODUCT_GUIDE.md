# QuoteRoom — Product & Sales Guide

Your single source of truth for explaining QuoteRoom: what it does, who it's for, what it costs, how to
demo it, what to say (in messages and out loud), and how to answer the usual questions honestly.

> **★ = new in the "quote to cash" release** (GST invoices, online approval, reminders, revisions, profit,
> reports, email). Don't promise ★ features to customers until that release is live.

---

## 1. QuoteRoom in one breath

**QuoteRoom is a quotation app for interior designers and furniture makers.** You measure each room in
feet and inches on your phone, QuoteRoom works out the square feet, applies your rates and gives you a
branded PDF to send on WhatsApp in minutes, then helps you get the job confirmed, invoiced and paid.

- **Short version (one line):** "Measure on site, send a professional quotation before you leave, and get paid faster."
- **For the owner:** "Your own branded quotation app, for your whole team, at one price."

---

## 2. Who it's for (and who it isn't)

**Best fit**
- Interior designers and design studios (1–10 people), modular kitchen and wardrobe firms, turnkey interior contractors.
- Furniture works / carpentry workshops that quote by square foot.
- Businesses that today quote in Excel, a notebook, or a Word/PDF template, and lose time and look less professional.

**Signs they need it**
- Quotes take hours or days after the site visit; customers go cold.
- Mistakes in sqft or totals; different staff quote different prices.
- No idea which jobs are confirmed, which are pending, and who still owes money.

**Not a fit (be honest)**
- Big fit-out companies that need procurement, inventory, vendor POs, attendance → that's an ERP (e.g. Dzylo).
- Businesses that only need a billing/accounting app (ledgers, GST returns) → Vyapar / Tally style tools.
- Anyone who needs to work fully offline: QuoteRoom needs an internet connection.

## 4. What it does — feature by feature

Explain features as **problem → what QuoteRoom does → what the customer gets**.

### Quoting
- **Feet & inches, room by room.** Add rooms (Master Bedroom, Kitchen…) and items (Wardrobe, Loft…). Enter
  length and width in ft and in; sqft, room totals and the grand total update as you type.
- **Your materials and rates.** Acrylic, Laminates, Veneer with Box Work / Frame Work rates, plus any
  materials they add themselves (Glass, PU, Wallpaper, Wooden Flooring…) with one rate per sqft. Hide old ones; rename freely.
- **Fittings & accessories.** A price list (Magic Corner, Tandem Drawer, hinges…) added by quantity × price.
- **Discounts** as a percentage or a fixed amount.
- **Plywood estimate.** Roughly how many 8×4 boards a job needs, and the plywood listed on the quote.
- **★ Units of measurement.** Settings → Units: sizes in feet & inches, mm, cm or inches, and areas & rates in sqft or
  sq m, for the whole team. The form, quotation page, PDFs, WhatsApp, customer link and new invoices all follow. Changing
  units never changes a measurement or a price (a 7 ft wardrobe just shows as 2134 mm). *Pitch: "Measure in mm? Set it once."*
- **★ Plywood pricing.** Admin → Rates → Plywood: your own plywood list (names you choose) with a price difference
  per sqft vs your default (e.g. 18mm BWP +150, 12mm MR −200). Material rates are for the default. Each quotation picks a
  plywood (default pre-selected), any item can use its own, and board materials (switch "On plywood" per material; Glass,
  Wallpaper etc. off) are priced at rate + difference. The customer sees the final rate, e.g. "Laminates – Box Work ·
  18mm BWP". Changing the default keeps every price; Costs has an optional extra cost per plywood for accurate profit.
  *Pitch: "Customer wants marine ply instead? Tap it, and the whole quotation re-prices."*
- **Auto-save.** A half-filled quotation is saved as a draft, so nothing is lost if the phone locks.
- **★ Revisions.** Every saved change becomes Rev 2, Rev 3…, with a list of what changed ("TV Unit 10 ft × 5 ft → 12 ft × 5 ft").
  The PDF shows the revision number.
  **Restore**: open any older revision → *Restore revision N* makes it the current quotation again, saved as a new
  revision ("restored from Rev 1"), so nothing is lost. Priced at today's rates (it warns if a rate changed). Admins only;
  not while an invoice is active (cancel it first).

### Sending
- **Two PDFs.**
  - **Full Quotation:** every room, item and measurement with amounts (for the detail-minded customer, or internal use).
  - **★ Initial Quotation, room by room** (default; Settings → Quotation PDFs): each room with what it's made of (your own
    description per room, one or more picked from a list you keep in Settings → Quotation PDFs → Room descriptions, or its
    materials), its items with sizes and No's, and **one amount per room**; each room's fittings
    as a "<Room> – Hardware" row. No rates or per-item prices, so it's harder to shop around. Then Basic Total, discount,
    Total and your numbered sections: Material Specifications, Payment Terms, Delivery Terms, Important Notes, T&C.
    (Option: "By material", amounts per material, as before.)
  - **★ No's:** a count per measured item (2 identical shutters on one line); area and price multiply by it.
- **WhatsApp in one tap.** A ready-made message with the room-wise summary, totals and payment details.
- **★ Online approval link.** The customer opens a link (no app, no login), sees the quotation in the
  business's colours, downloads the PDF and taps **Accept** or **Request changes**. Accepting moves the job
  to Confirmed and notifies the owner. The person sending chooses what the customer sees: **material
  breakdown** or **full detailed quotation**. You can see when and how often they opened it.
- **★ Email.** Send the quotation (Initial or Full PDF attached, with the accept-online link) from the
  business's own email account.

### Getting paid
- **Payment details on every PDF** (UPI ID, bank account).
- **Record payments** (advance, instalments) and see paid vs balance on each quotation.
- **Razorpay payment links** for the full balance or a part (advance, stage payment). Paid links are recorded automatically (and a receipt is emailed when email is set up).
- **★ Payment reminders.** One tap opens WhatsApp with a polite reminder: amount due, what's been paid, UPI/bank details and optionally a payment link.
- **★ Balances due.** One page listing every customer who still owes money, biggest first, with when they were last reminded.

### ★ Invoices
- **Turn a confirmed quotation into an invoice in one step.**
- **With GST** (tax invoice): CGST + SGST for customers in the same state, IGST for other states (worked
  out from the business's GSTIN and the place of supply); customer GSTIN for business customers; HSN/SAC; prices before or including GST.
- **Without GST:** a plain invoice with no GST anywhere on it.
- **Room by room** (every item with its size) or **summary by material**.
- Numbered per financial year (INV/2026-27/001…); cancelled invoices keep their number; amount in words; no signature needed.
- **Shareable online** like the quotation: a private link, WhatsApp and email.
- Once invoiced, the balance due uses the invoice total.

### Running the business
- **Job stages:** Awaiting confirmation → Confirmed → Ongoing → Completed (plus On hold, Cancelled), with history and notes.
- **Dashboard:** value of work at each stage.
- **Team:** staff logins; staff see only the quotations they're given; admins see everything.
- **★ Costs & profit (owner only):** enter what materials and fittings cost you; each quotation shows its
  estimated profit and margin **before GST**. Never shown to staff or customers, never printed.
- **★ Reports (owner only):** this financial year (won, collected, invoiced, GST, profit, to collect), a
  projection to 31 March, month by month, every financial year and "till date".

### Their brand, their app
- **Their own web address** (e.g. `sharma.quote-room.co.in`), their logo, colours, fonts and header style on the app, PDFs and customer pages.
- **Company name, GSTIN and logo lock** once saved, so nobody can quietly change them (support unlocks if needed).
- Works in the browser on any phone, tablet or laptop; add it to the home screen for one-tap access. Nothing to install.

### Safety
- Each paying business has **its own app and its own database**; trials are kept separately. Other customers never see their data.
- Customer details, amounts, rates and payment details are **encrypted** in the database; passwords are hashed; HTTPS everywhere.
- On request we can export their data (spreadsheet files).

## 5. How to demo it (10 minutes)

**Before:** open the live demo (the "Try the live demo" button on quote-room.co.in), or their own trial.

1. **Ask first (1 min):** "How do you make quotes today? How long after the site visit does the customer get it?"
2. **Make a quote live (3 min):** New Quote → customer name → room "Master Bedroom" → Wardrobe 8 ft × 7 ft, Laminates Box Work →
   Loft 8 × 2 → add a Magic Corner. *Say:* "Watch the total, it updates as I type. That's the quote done on site."
3. **Show both PDFs (1 min):** Full vs Initial. *Say:* "Send the Initial first; it shows the price without giving away your measurements."
4. **Send it (1 min):** WhatsApp button. ★ Then the approval link: "Your customer taps Accept, and you get notified."
5. **Get paid (2 min):** Record an advance → balance updates → payment link. ★ Remind button, Balances page, Create invoice.
6. **Their brand (1 min):** Settings → logo and colours. *Say:* "This becomes your app, at your own web address."
7. **Close (1 min):** "Let's set up your free trial today; you'll have your own app within a day."

**Demo tips**
- Use *their* rates and a room *they* quoted recently; it lands much harder than sample data.
- Keep it on a phone if they work on phones. Let them type one item themselves.
- Don't show every setting. Show: quote → send → paid.

## 11. Glossary (so you explain things the same way every time)

- **Full Quotation:** PDF with every room, item, measurement and amount.
- **Initial Quotation:** PDF room by room: items with sizes and No's, one amount per room, Hardware rows (or by material).
- **Material breakdown / Full detailed:** ★ what the customer sees on the approval link (same idea as the two PDFs).
- **Won:** a quotation that reached Confirmed (or later) and wasn't cancelled.
- **Invoiced:** ★ issued invoices (with or without GST), not cancelled. **GST:** only the GST on GST invoices.
- **Profit:** ★ price before GST minus the business's own costs; estimated, using today's costs.
- **Balance due:** what's left to collect: the invoice total once invoiced, otherwise the quotation total, minus payments.
- **Read-only:** after a plan ends (and the grace period for paid plans): can log in, view and download, can't create or edit.
