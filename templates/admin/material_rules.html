Material rules

Rates
These work out how much to buy for each quotation's material list, e.g. 1 sheet of plywood for every 9 {{ area_label }} of Box Work. You don't have to change anything here: the starting numbers are typical. Check them once with your carpenter if you like.
How much you use
Pick Box Work or Frame Work for the items you measure on a quotation, or one of your BOQ lines (Painting, Floor tiles…): a BOQ line with rules is replaced on the list by what it needs. In a name, [ply] is the ply chosen on the quotation and [finish] the item's finish (e.g. Laminates). Lines with the same name add up across the quotation.
