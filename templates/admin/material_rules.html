Material rules

Rates
Each quotation has a Material list: everything to buy for the job, by group, to send to your suppliers. These rules work out the board, laminate, edge banding and so on from each item's area: for example 0.11 sheets of plywood for every {{ area_label }} of Box Work, plus wastage. The starting numbers are estimates: check them once with your carpenter and change them to how you work. Fittings and BOQ lines go on the list as they are on the quotation.
Rules for measured items

An item's name can use {material} (the line's material, e.g. Laminates) and {plywood} (its plywood). Lines with the same item and unit add up across the quotation. Round up gives whole sheets, rolls or boxes.
