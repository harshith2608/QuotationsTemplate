How Rates Work

Each item's cost = Area × Rate for that material + work combination.

Item Cost = Area × Rate(Material, Work Type)

Example — Wardrobe: 10 {{ units.area_label() }}, Laminates + Box Work @ ₹850{{ units.rate_label() }}:

10 × ₹850 = ₹8,500

Fittings & accessories cost = Quantity × Price per unit, e.g. 4 Tandem Drawers @ ₹4,500:

4 × ₹4,500 = ₹18,000
