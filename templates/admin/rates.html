Item options

When the same item costs more in another finish, board, brand… Make a section for each (e.g. Finish: Laminate, Acrylic +25% · Board: Century Sainik, Century Bond +₹400 per unit). In each section the first option is your base: your items' rates below are for it. Every other option adds a % of that rate or a ₹ amount per unit (sqft, rft, nos…); a cheaper one can take off (−100). Tick each section on the items that come in it; on a quotation those items get one choice per section and the rate changes by itself. Base options aren't printed; others are ("Wardrobe (Acrylic finish, Century Bond)"). Tick Choose per quotation on a section to pick it once at the top of each quotation (e.g. this home is on Century Bond): every line in it follows, and any line can still be changed on its own.

How Rates Work

Each item's cost = Area × Rate for that material + work combination.

Item Cost = Area × Rate(Material, Work Type)

Example — Wardrobe: 10 {{ units.area_label() }}, Laminates + Box Work @ ₹850{{ units.rate_label() }}:

10 × ₹850 = ₹8,500

Fittings & accessories cost = Quantity × Price per unit, e.g. 4 Tandem Drawers @ ₹4,500:

4 × ₹4,500 = ₹18,000
