Email Notifications

When a customer pays through a Razorpay payment link, the app emails a receipt to the customer (if their email is on the quotation) and an alert to you. Emails are sent from your own email account.

{% if email_ready %}Email is set up {% else %}Not set up yet{% endif %}
Set up with Gmail (about 5 minutes) ShowHide
  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification

    Open myaccount.google.com/security with the Gmail account you want to send from, and turn on 2-Step Verification. Google requires this before you can create an App Password.

  2. Create an App Password

    Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, type a name such as Quotation app and click Create. Copy the 16-letter password Google shows. Use this, not your normal Gmail password.

  3. Fill in the form below

    Click Use Gmail settings to fill in the server details, then enter your Gmail address as the username, paste the App Password, and enter the email where you want payment alerts.

  4. Save and send a test

    Click Save Email Settings, then Send test email above. If it doesn't arrive in a minute, check the spam folder. If there's a problem, the message at the top of the page tells you what to fix.

Using another provider? Choose it below, or ask your email provider for its SMTP details. Use port 587 (or 465 if your provider says SSL).
