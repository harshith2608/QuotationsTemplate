Material list

{{ 'On' if mat_on else 'Off' }} for every trial and paying customer in the shared app. When on, every quotation has a Material list button: everything to buy for the job with wastage, by group, to copy or send on WhatsApp, and admins get Rates → Material rules. Turning it off hides it everywhere within a minute; lists and rules already made are kept. Needs the latest database update (Updates → Update all). A business on its own app (not the shared one) turns it on with MATERIAL_LIST=1.

Call bookings

Trials see Get your rates set up for you on their quotations and at the end of the guided setup: Book a free call (a 10-minute call in a half-hour slot, at least an hour ahead, up to a week) or Chat on WhatsApp (SUPPORT_WHATSAPP on the trials project). A booked call goes on the Schedule and the trial is marked hot. Your free times, India time: one or more ranges a day, like 09:00-10:15, 12:00-20:00; empty = not that day. Booked slots are left out by themselves.
