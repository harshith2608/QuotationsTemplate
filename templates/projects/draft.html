Draft from a message

Paste what the customer sent you (their WhatsApp message) or your notes from the site visit. We draft the quotation with your own items and your rates: you then check it, type the sizes that weren't given and save it as usual.

Any language mix is fine. Sizes in your own unit (ft, mm, cm or inches) are picked up for the items they belong to; a room's size isn't used as an item's size.
Takes about 10–20 seconds. Nothing is saved until you save the quotation. {{ left }} draft{{ '' if left == 1 else 's' }} left ({{ limit }}).
The message is read by our AI provider (Anthropic) and isn't stored or used to train AI. Rates always come from your own rates, never from the AI.
