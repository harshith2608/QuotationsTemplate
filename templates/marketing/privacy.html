Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ legal_updated }}

In short: we collect only what we need to run {{ product }} and talk to you about it. Each business's quotation data is kept separate from every other business. We never sell personal data, and you can ask us to see, correct or delete yours at any time.

Who we are

{{ legal_name }} ("we", "us") provides {{ product }}, a quotation and project management app for interior design and furniture businesses. This policy covers our website and the {{ product }} app we set up for each customer business. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

Two kinds of data, two roles

  • Your data as our customer (the business owner and your staff): we decide how this is used, so we are the Data Fiduciary for it.
  • Data you enter about your own customers (their names, mobile numbers, addresses, quotations and payments): you decide how this is used. Your business is the Data Fiduciary and we act only as your Data Processor, handling it on your instructions to provide the service.

What we collect

  • When you request a free trial: the name, company name, mobile number, city, email (if you give one) and type of business you enter in the trial form, and the ad or page you came from (for example "utm_source=facebook"). We use these only to set up your trial and contact you about it. If you don't become a customer, we delete them within 12 months.
  • When you contact us: your name, phone number, business name and anything you tell us on WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • When you subscribe: business details for setup and invoicing (company name, address, GSTIN, logo, contact person) and payment records. We do not store card or bank login details.
  • When you and your staff use the app: usernames, display names, an encrypted (hashed) password, and records of who created or edited a quotation and when.
  • On our website: standard technical data such as browser type and pages visited, and, only if you accept cookies, the advertising data described under "Cookies and the Meta Pixel" below.

Cookies and the Meta Pixel

Our website uses the Meta Pixel (from Meta Platforms, the company behind Facebook and Instagram) and, where enabled, Google Ads tags. They help us see which of our ads bring visitors and trial requests, so we spend less on ads that don't work.

  • They only load if you tap "Accept" on the cookie notice. If you decline or ignore it, they are not loaded and set no cookies.
  • Once accepted, they can collect the pages you visit on this site, the buttons you tap (for example "Start free trial" or a WhatsApp button), whether you sent a trial request, your browser and device type, and your IP address. They set cookies such as Meta's _fbp to recognise your browser on later visits.
  • Meta and Google may link this to your Facebook, Instagram or Google account if you are signed in, and use it under their own privacy policies (Meta, Google). We never send them the name or number you enter in the trial form.
  • You can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page, and control ad personalisation in your Facebook and Google ad settings.

The {{ product }} app itself (the quotation app we set up for each business) does not use advertising cookies. It only uses the cookies needed to keep you signed in.

Tutorial videos. The videos on our Tutorials page are hosted on YouTube. The page shows their preview images from YouTube's servers; the YouTube player itself loads only when you tap play, in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and YouTube may then set cookies under Google's privacy policy.

How we use it

  • To set up, run, secure and support your {{ product }} app.
  • To reply to enquiries, arrange demos and send service messages, such as setup and renewal reminders.
  • To bill you and keep records required by tax law.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.

We process your personal data with your consent or for these legitimate uses permitted under the DPDP Act. You can withdraw consent for marketing at any time by telling us; it won't affect the service you've paid for.

Who we share it with

We never sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run {{ product }}, under their own security and privacy commitments:

  • Hosting and database providers, such as Vercel and Neon, which store and serve the app. Data may be stored on servers outside India, for example in Singapore.
  • Email delivery providers used by your app's notification settings.
  • Payment providers you choose to connect (for example Razorpay). Customer payments go directly to your own account; we don't handle that money.
  • Advertising providers (Meta and Google), only for website visitors who accept cookies, as described under "Cookies and the Meta Pixel".

We may also disclose data if required by law or a valid order from an Indian authority.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and app data while your trial or plan is active. If a trial or plan ends and you don't subscribe or renew, your app becomes read-only and your data is permanently deleted 30 days after the end date. You can ask for an export before then. Copies in backups are removed as those backups expire. We keep invoices and payment records for as long as tax law requires.

How we protect it

Each paying business gets its own app and its own database; during a free trial your data is kept in its own separate space (a database schema) in a database shared with other trials, and is never visible to them. Your customers' details, quotation amounts, rates, payments and payment-gateway secrets are encrypted in the database with a key kept separately from it, so a copy of the database alone can't be read. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and access to production systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it's used;
  • ask us to correct, complete, update or delete it;
  • withdraw consent you've given;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you; and
  • raise a grievance with us, and if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

If you're a customer of a business that uses {{ product }}, please contact that business first. They control your data, and we'll help them respond.

Children

{{ product }} is a business tool and isn't meant for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect children's personal data.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we'll update the date at the top and tell active customers by WhatsApp or email.
