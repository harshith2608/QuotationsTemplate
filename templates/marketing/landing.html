    FAQ

    Questions owners ask us

    Do I need a computer?

    No. {{ product }} runs in the browser on any smartphone, tablet or laptop. There is nothing to install.

    Do my customers need to install anything?

    No. They receive a PDF on WhatsApp or email, or a link to view and accept the quotation in their browser.

    How does the free trial work?

    Request a free trial and we'll set up your own {{ product }} within a day, at your own web address. Use it on real jobs for 7 days, with every feature. No payment details needed. If you subscribe, everything you've set up stays exactly where it is.

    How is this different from getting a website made?

    A custom quotation website usually costs ₹30,000–40,000 up front, and hosting, fixes and new features cost extra. {{ product }} costs less per year and includes hosting, updates, new features and support, and you can try it free before paying anything.

    Is my data private?

    Yes. Paying customers get their own app and their own separate database, and trials are kept in their own separate space. Other {{ product }} customers never see your quotations or customers.

    Can my staff use it?

    Yes, as many as you need, at no extra cost: the price is for your whole business, not per user. Staff see the quotations you give them access to; admins see everything, including costs and profit.

    Can I change my rates and terms later?

    Anytime. Rates, terms and conditions, material specifications, payment details and branding are all editable from the admin settings.

    What happens when my plan or trial ends?

    We remind you before it ends. Paid plans get 7 extra days to renew. After that your app becomes read-only: you can still sign in, view quotations and download PDFs. If you don't renew, your data is permanently deleted 30 days after your plan or trial ended.

    Can I get a refund?

    Plans are prepaid and not refundable, which is why the 7-day trial is free. Try everything first and only pay when you're sure.
